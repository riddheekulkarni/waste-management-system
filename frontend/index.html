How EcoClean Civic Works

From initial citizen report to on-site clearance, every stage is verified and tracked.

01

Report Issue

Snap a photo and pinpoint the street location on our interactive civic map. No complex forms required.

02

AI Understands

YOLOv8 computer vision classifies waste types, computes coverage area, and checks for nearby duplicates.

03

Municipality Acts

The ticket is instantly dispatched to Sanitation, Recycling, or Public Works with severity priority.

04

Track Progress

Follow live progress in your dashboard with real-time Server-Sent Events from triage to final clearance.

Core Capabilities

Built for Modern Civic Operations

Practical, reliable civic technology designed to keep communities clean and accountable.

🤖

AI-Assisted Detection

Real-time computer vision recognizes recyclable items, organic waste, plastics, and hazardous materials.

📍

Location-Aware Reporting

Instant GPS location detection with reverse street geocoding and landmark verification on Leaflet maps.

🏛️

Automated Department Routing

Canonical ontology maps detected waste to Sanitation, Recycling, or Hazmat without administrative delays.

🔄

Proximity Duplicate Detection

Scans a 50-meter radius to link related complaints, avoiding duplicate field crew dispatches.

⚡

Real-Time Event Streaming

Live status updates stream directly to your browser via Server-Sent Events (SSE) as jobs execute.

📱

Mobile-Optimized Experience

Engineered for seamless mobile reporting on the go with camera capture and accessible touch targets.
